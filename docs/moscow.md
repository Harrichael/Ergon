# MoSCoW prioritization

## Goal

This master list sorts our work into the four MoSCoW buckets. It gives us:

1. Clarity of priorities and scope until launch.
2. A well-defined **Minimal Shippable Product** that:
   - is excellent, and
   - we are very confident will be done **before** launch.
3. A clear home or path forward for every line item or idea in this project.

If this document succeeds, you will be able to:

1. Know which of the four buckets any Jira ticket or loose thread belongs in.
2. Prioritize your work and your path forward.
3. See a shared path to success for the product.

## The buckets

| Bucket | In short |
| --- | --- |
| [Must Have](#must-have) | The Minimal Shippable Product. Launch waits for it. |
| [Should Have](#should-have) | Committed, but it doesn't put launch at risk. |
| [Could Have](#could-have) | Maybe, but unclear. Uncommitted. |
| [Won't Have](#wont-have) | Not now, maybe later. Out of scope. |

### Must Have

**The Minimal Shippable Product.**

We can ship this, and users and stakeholders will be happy, even if
high-priority follow-ons remain. It satisfies security requirements and
protects the business's image, including excellence and polish. It may still
contain:

- manual work in a back room for certain processes;
- temporary scaffolding that later phases will make obsolete;
- significant scope reduction, even where users can see it.

**The bar to get in is strict.** Ask:

1. If this line item were missing, would we delay launch?
2. Are we okay building this line item and **not** having a working product
   by the due date?

### Should Have

**Should, with a capital S.** We are committed to this work, but it doesn't
put launch at risk.

Items here must be:

- unambiguous and well defined;
- about de-risking launch, to ensure success.

These don't disqualify an item:

- It may be required for long-term product success. The test: can we get it
  done after launch, before it risks project failure?
- It is a user-facing feature.
- It is a critical architectural or non-functional requirement.

This bucket is **not** for:

- a wish list;
- work that mainly serves other projects. Tackle and prioritize that with
  those projects.

### Could Have

**Maybe, but unclear.**

Significant ambiguity makes these items uncommitted. The ambiguity may be
around requirements, scope, product-market fit, user expectations, business
needs, capacity, or technical implementation. Every item here will be:

- evaluated **after** launch;
- explicitly accepted **or** rejected.

### Won't Have

**Not now, but maybe later. Out of scope.**

Picking these up requires future projects, efforts, or product iteration
cycles.
