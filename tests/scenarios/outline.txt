# applied back in one transaction.

# Each row: slug, status, ~status, priority, ~priority, size, ~size, deps,
# ~blocked, then the title. `-` is an empty cell; `~` cells are calculated.
# Titles show the nesting: the root's is bare, the rest indent under it.
